Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 2026

TulipLock is designed to protect your files by performing all encryption and decryption directly on your device. Your files and passwords never leave your device.

Information Collection

  • TulipLock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • Your files are processed only on your device.
  • Your passwords are never stored, uploaded, or recoverable.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Permissions

Storage Access (Storage Access Framework)

Used only to allow you to select files and choose where encrypted or decrypted files are saved.

Foreground Service

Used to keep encryption or decryption running safely while displaying progress.

Notifications (Android 13+)

Required only for foreground service notifications while processing files.

Internet

  • TulipLock does not require an internet connection.
  • No files, passwords, or personal information are uploaded to any server.

Security

  • AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • Unique random salt and nonce generated for every file.
  • The original filename is securely stored inside the encrypted file and restored when the file is decrypted.
  • Built-in authentication detects incorrect passwords and modified files.

Data Sharing

  • TulipLock does not sell, share, or transfer your personal information or files to third parties.

Children's Privacy

TulipLock does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.

Important

Passwords are never stored and cannot be recovered. Please keep your password in a safe place.
